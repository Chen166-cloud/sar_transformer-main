Qualitative comparison on a single UCMerced buildings scene. Panels are arranged in row-major order: (a) Clean, (b) Noisy, (c) LEE (Lee-MMSE), (d) SAR-BM3D, (e) SAR-CAM, (f) Trans-SAR, (g) Ours, and (h) Ours ratio. All panels show the same field of view and retain their original grayscale renderings; (h) is a ratio visualization and is interpreted separately from the restored images.
